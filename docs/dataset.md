# Notes on the dataset

Dataset: IST (International Stroke Trial)

When: 1992-1996

What for:  to establish whether early administration of aspirin, heparin, both or neither influenced the clinical course of acute ischaemic stroke.

Patients: 19.435 patients with onset within the previous 48 hours and no clear indication for, or clear contraindication to, treatment with aspirin or subcutaneous heparin.

The data are collected in 3 different moments:
- right before the treatment (A), i.e. at randomization
- after 14 days, or at dimission (B)
- after 6 months (C)

In (A) the following data are collected:
- age, 
- gender, 
- time from onset to randomisation, 
- presence or absence of atrial fibrillation (AF), 
- aspirin administration within 3 days prior to randomisation, 
- systolic blood pressure at randomisation, 
- level of consciousness
-  neurological deficit.
--> the deficit can either be:
1) total anterior circulation syndrome (TACS), 
2) partial anterior circulation syndrome (PACS), 
3) posterior circulation syndrome (POCS) and lacunar syndrome (LACS)

- Treatment

In (B) the following data are collected:
- the occurrence of recurrent stroke, 
- pulmonary embolism, 
- and death (date and cause of death)
- the treatement actually given to the patient in the 2 weeks
- final diagnosis
- dimission informations

In (C) the following data are collected:
- degree of recovery, 
- place of residence and current use of antiplatelet or anticoagulant drugs,
- death (date and cause of death).
- place of residence
--> cause of death can be:
1) initial stroke, 
2) recurrent ischaemic stroke, 
3) recurrent haemorrhagic stroke, 
4) pneumonia, 
5) coronary artery disease, 
6) pulmonary embolism, 
7) other vascular cause
8) nonvascular cause
--> place of residence can be:
1) own home, 
2) relatives home, 
3) residential care,
4) nursing home,
5) other hospital departments 
6) unknown

EXTRA: a number of derived quantities was obtained after the trial.
